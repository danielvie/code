# Convergence method

## 1. Frame the problem

Read the existing project artifacts according to [artifacts.md](artifacts.md). Establish the intended outcome, important terms, known constraints, and the decision or understanding being sought.

Challenge statements that conflict with the project's canonical goal, language, or architecture decisions. Do not repeat settled context unless needed to expose a contradiction or prevent misunderstanding.

## 2. Gather evidence

Inspect the codebase and available sources before asking questions they can answer. Research current or specialized knowledge when tools are available. State when evidence or domain knowledge is insufficient.

Distinguish established facts from inferences, assumptions, preferences, and unknowns.

## 3. Develop the shared model

Combine the user's context with relevant evidence and domain knowledge. Contribute:

- insights and established practices
- plausible alternatives
- material tradeoffs and consequences
- contradictions and risks
- concrete recommendations and rationale

Challenge both the user's reasoning and the current recommendation when warranted.

## 4. Close gaps

Use focused questions to expose assumptions, clarify reasoning, test conclusions, and resolve dependencies between decisions.

Prefer one primary question at a time. For every question, include the recommended answer and why it is recommended. Avoid turning the discussion into an interrogation.

Prioritize the question most likely to change the shared model or recommendation.

## 5. Resolve differences

Identify whether disagreement comes from different facts, assumptions, constraints, terminology, priorities, or risk tolerance.

Convergence means shared understanding, not mandatory agreement. Preserve a clearly understood disagreement when evidence cannot resolve a difference in priorities or preference.

## 6. Stop

Convergence is reached when:

- the problem and intended outcome are understood
- relevant constraints and assumptions are explicit
- material alternatives and tradeoffs have been considered
- the recommendation or decision has clear rationale
- remaining uncertainty or disagreement is explicit
- no open question is likely to materially change the conclusion

Before stopping, update any canonical engineering artifact whose truth changed, following [artifacts.md](artifacts.md). Do not create a separate convergence summary.
